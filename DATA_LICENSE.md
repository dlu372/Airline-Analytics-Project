The source files `data/raw/airports.dat`, `data/raw/airlines.dat`, and `data/raw/routes.dat` are from the OpenFlights Airport, Airline, and Route databases. Processed and analytics outputs in this repository are generated from those source databases. To the extent an output constitutes or contains an applicable derived database or database content, the OpenFlights ODbL 1.0, DbCL 1.0, and any relevant source-specific terms continue to apply. The MIT License does not replace those terms.

## Attribution

This project uses the [OpenFlights Airport, Airline, and Route databases](https://openflights.org/data.php). The route data is treated as a historical June 2014 snapshot. Exact source-file hashes are recorded in `data/raw/checksums.sha256`.

OpenFlights identifies upstream sources including OurAirports, DAFIF, Wikipedia, and Airline Route Mapper. Refer to the OpenFlights data page and the linked upstream sources for their notices and applicable terms.
